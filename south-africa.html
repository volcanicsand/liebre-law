Before you reach out

A few clear answers.

Can I apply without paying a lawyer?

Yes. Access to the U.S. Refugee Admissions Program and its application and processing services are free. Official referral partners provide assistance. A consultation with Liebre Law is optional and separate from that process.

Who is this consultation for?

South African families considering the Afrikaner refugee pathway who want independent legal advice. Current program guidance includes South African nationals of Afrikaner ethnicity or another racial minority who are living in South Africa and can articulate a qualifying persecution claim. Individual eligibility and admission are determined by the U.S. government.

Does the fee include an application?

No. US$150 covers one 30-minute attorney consultation. It does not include preparing or filing an application, interview attendance, or ongoing representation. Any additional work requires a separate agreement.

Can Liebre Law get my case approved faster?

No. We do not offer priority access, expedited processing, or guaranteed results. Liebre Law is independent of the U.S. government and the official referral partners.

What should I send in my first email?

Your name, time zone, and a few times you are available. Please do not send identity documents, detailed persecution accounts, or other sensitive information in your initial scheduling email.
